## Experience

**Researcher, Stanford IRIS Lab**

- **Human-out-of-the-loop Robot Learning** (Jun. 2026 - Present). Developing a task-and-scene proposal pipeline for autonomous self-play, enabling robot policies to acquire diverse manipulation skills with minimal human supervision. Designing a framework for continuous skill acquisition — day-time autonomous exploration and reset, night-time trajectory-based training — across a multi-arm setup.
- **Correction and Reinforcement from Evidence** (May 2026 - Present). Developed a data collection strategy prioritizing failure-distribution coverage and counterfactual demonstrations to optimize policy training efficiency, and designed a dual-signal training loop combining human corrections and policy rollouts to jointly update a policy actor and an Implicit Q-Learning (IQL) critic.
- **Learning in Partially Observable Environments** (Oct. 2025 - Present). Investigated policy training under partial observability, addressing self-occlusion failure modes such as an arm blocking the camera line-of-sight during manipulation.

**Independent Research**

- **Humanoid Robots** (Jan. 2026 - Present). Training a Unitree G1 humanoid to play table tennis at a competitive level via human demonstration and reinforcement learning with sim-to-real transfer, alongside other sport and movement skills including football throwing, soccer kicking, and dancing.
- **Spiking Neural Networks** (Apr. 2026 - Present). Extended Hodgkin-Huxley neuron dynamics to model biological neurogenesis, enabling a spiking neural network to grow and prune neurons over time as it learns. Cut catastrophic forgetting by ~3x versus a fixed-size network by protecting mature neurons during continual learning, validated via an ablation study.
- **Quantum Optical Convolutional Neural Networks** (Jun. 2019 - Mar. 2020). Created a novel framework to simulate the training of convolutional neural networks on quantum computing systems, demonstrating >100x boosts in computational and memory efficiency over a classical CNN.

**Leadership and Industry**

- **Co-President, Stanford Student Robotics Club** (Sep. 2025 - Jun. 2026). Led student engagement with robotics on campus by organizing project showcases, company events, and hackathons, growing participation and industry partnerships across the club.
- **Software Lead, Stanford Autonomous Underwater Vehicle Project** (Oct. 2024 - Jun. 2026). Led the design and implementation of a ROS 2 based autonomy stack integrating perception, localization, and path-planning, and managed a cross-disciplinary software team of ~15 engineers delivering a field-validated robotics platform for competitions and scientific missions.
- **Software Engineering Intern, AMD AI Group** (Jun. 2024 - Sep. 2025). Specialized in machine learning compilers, optimizing model performance across diverse hardware architectures and reducing latency in large-scale deployments. Contributed to Wave, a GPU domain-specific language, by designing and extending custom kernels.

**Research Intern, Stanford University**

- **California Wildfire Prediction System** (Jun. 2022 - Jan. 2024). Developed a wildfire prediction system based on a novel machine-learning framework, successfully predicting 85% of major wildfires in California up to 2 weeks in advance, and compiled a comprehensive wildfire dataset with >37 million data points.
- **AI-assisted Facial Surgery Planning and Evaluation** (Jun. 2021 - May 2022). Developed AI algorithms to aid surgical planning for correcting facial structural deformities that cause debilitating breathing, sleeping, and sensory disorders, achieving >95% accuracy in classifying patient conditions.
- **COPD Exacerbation Prediction** (Jun. 2020 - May 2021). Developed a multi-modal solution for predicting the climate's impact on exacerbation risks of respiratory diseases, built on a new architecture combining convolutional and recurrent neural networks with 93% cough detection accuracy.
